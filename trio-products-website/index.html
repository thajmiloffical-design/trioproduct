About Trio Products

Trio Products is a smart retail technology startup focused on solving supermarket billing problems using Artificial Intelligence, IoT, and automation. Our mission is to reduce billing queues and create a smooth shopping experience.

Our Product

AI Product Detection

Camera detects products placed inside the basket using computer vision.

Weight Verification

Load cell sensor checks the product weight to prevent wrong billing.

Automatic Billing

The product is automatically added to the customer’s bill.

UPI Payment

Customer can scan QR code and complete payment quickly.
